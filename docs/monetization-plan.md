## Measurement

Use the existing TGIR collector and course reports, without another analytics service:

1. **External sessions:** exclude identifiable owner, QA and deployment traffic; retain an “unknown” category where it cannot be separated. Do not count every hash change as a new visit.
2. **Engaged sessions:** proposed definition is at least 60 seconds of active exhibit use plus a meaningful control interaction. Add the necessary event if approved; the current tracker does not establish this definition. Report a time/scroll proxy separately meanwhile.
3. **Course interest:** unique sessions clicking a course link ÷ engaged sessions. Preserve incoming acquisition campaigns and record the outbound course/placement separately; check attribution across the two domains rather than assuming their browser cookies join.
4. **Earnings:** reconcile actual instructor referral/coupon reports with the Observatory campaign. UTM tags alone do not establish Udemy purchase attribution. Keep unmatched sales unassigned and separate provisional sales, refunds and retained earnings.
5. **Efficiency:** retained attributable earnings ÷ engaged sessions; contribution after incremental generation, review, delivery and promotion costs. Include recorded owner time when assessing profitability. Review after the applicable refund/reporting period.

Avoid small-sample A/B tests and automatic expansion based on a few clicks. Weekly reporting should answer: where did useful visits come from, what did they do, what sold, and what did it cost?

## Economics and spending

**Illustration, not a forecast:** for the three in-scope courses only, assume 5% of engaged sessions click a course, 4% of those clicks become retained purchases, and average retained instructor earnings are **$40 per purchase**. Actual prices, conversion and earnings are unverified; no VaR sales are included.

| Engaged sessions per month | Retained course purchases | Instructor earnings before production costs and income tax |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 2 | $80 |
| 5,000 | 10 | $400 |
| 10,000 | 20 | $800 |

At those assumptions, $1,000/month needs approximately 12,500 engaged sessions. Early revenue may be zero. An illustrative $100 exhibit production cost needs 1,250 incremental engaged sessions to recover at $0.08/session, before other costs. Replace these assumptions with measured results before setting a revenue target or buying traffic.

Udemy states instructor-promoted purchases earn **97% of Net Amount**, versus 37% for sales without instructor promotion. This is not 97% of the displayed checkout price. Its referral guidance specifies a 24-hour purchase window. Verify the current account-generated link and reporting; an expired coupon must not promise a discount. [Revenue share](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/229605008-Instructor-Revenue-Share), [referral rules](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/229603968-Promote-Your-Course-With-Coupons-and-Referral-Links).

Keep the current Cloudflare static architecture within its [Free limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/) and reuse the existing collector. Target **$0 additional recurring software spend** initially. Domain renewal, existing DigitalOcean costs, model generation and QA still exist. Record per-exhibit costs; no paid campaigns or new paid subscriptions are proposed for the first experiment.

Ads are supplementary: an assumed $5 page RPM produces $50 at 10,000 monetized page views; this is a sensitivity example, not an industry benchmark. Page views and engaged sessions are different measures. Approval requires original, useful content; personalized Google ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland require a Google-certified CMP integrated with TCF. Keep ads disabled until actual approval/configuration and consent verification. [AdSense eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724), [CMP requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116).

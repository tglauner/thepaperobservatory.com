# The Paper Observatory product direction

The Paper Observatory, intended for **thepaperobservatory.com**, turns SSRN research into interactive explanations. The content, code, design and derivative learning material are generated with AI. The original authors retain credit for the research; AI-generated companions do not imply author endorsement or independent peer review.

Finance is the main editorial focus: risk, portfolios, market structure, econometrics and the mechanics behind quantitative methods. Exceptional papers from other fields qualify when interaction reveals an idea substantially better than a conventional summary. The library serves strong undergraduate readers and early PhD students through Foundation and Advanced views.

## The reader experience

The entrance helps readers choose a question, not navigate a directory of PDFs. Each card states the paper, the mechanism to explore, the topic and the expected mathematical depth. Every companion preserves shared navigation, source attribution, related reading and a route back to the library. The central exhibit remains specific to the source.

A good session moves from a concrete question to a manipulated model, an observed consequence, an explanation and a qualified conclusion. Formal detail follows intuition. Evidence pages distinguish the paper's results, exact educational reconstructions and new synthetic demonstrations. Quiz and glossary tools reinforce understanding without requiring an account.

## Generation and publication

Initial design uses the owner's current preference, GPT-6.1 Sol with Ultra reasoning, when available in the cloud agent. Record the actual model and setting; a preference is not evidence of the runtime used. Subsequent build, verification and publishing use the lowest-complexity tooling appropriate to the task.

The durable source is an open GitHub repository. Generation runs in cloud workspaces/agents, CI builds a reproducible static artifact, and Cloudflare is the hosting target. Local preview remains available for fast review. This user-selected target supersedes the Architecture Library's default DigitalOcean hosting without discarding its agent, privacy, testing or documentation standards.

Every addition carries source metadata, provenance, mathematical tests, browser evidence and an identifiable cloud job/commit. Preserve source PDFs privately unless redistribution is explicitly cleared. Public source links remain canonical SSRN links when the PDF cannot be republished.

## Editorial and commercial priorities

Choose papers for a real educational payoff, not an automated publication quota. Start with a small, accurate finance library. Expand through related concepts so every exhibit has a next learning step. Monetization combines relevant training, display advertising after approval, and source-faithful short-form discovery; see [monetization](monetization.md).

Useful operating measures are completed lab interactions, return visits, qualified course clicks and conversions, traffic from canonical social CTAs, revenue per session and generation/verification cost per published exhibit. Do not present these as tracked or achieved before the measurement system and consent model exist.

An AI-generated brand depends on visible quality: accurate source links, transparent modeling boundaries, original interactive explanations and corrections. Avoid manufactured testimonials, traffic statistics, fake accounts or unsupported revenue promises.

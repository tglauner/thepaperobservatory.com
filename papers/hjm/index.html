A guide to the HJM forward-rate framework

What does HJM model?

Heath, Jarrow and Morton model the evolution of the entire instantaneous forward-rate curve. The experiment starts from an illustrative curve and lets you compare one- and two-factor volatility shapes.

Why is HJM drift linked to volatility?

Under the risk-neutral measure and the model's assumptions, the no-arbitrage condition constrains forward-rate drift once the volatility loadings are specified. It does not determine the volatility model or remove risk premia from real-world expected returns.

What can the experiment show?

Change the starting curve and volatility shape, then inspect a synthetic quarter-year move. The paths are teaching scenarios, not a market calibration, a forecast, or the authors' empirical results.
